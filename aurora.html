Viewing tips · Cumberland NS

  • Kp 4–5 — possible faint glow low on the northern horizon under dark clear skies.
  • Kp 6+ — solid chance of visible aurora; stronger storms bring colour and structure.
  • Best window — 10 pm to 2 am Atlantic time.
  • Moon — new or thin crescent is ideal; full moon washes faint displays.
  • Location — get away from town lights, open northern horizon (Springhill / Oxford / Amherst corridor works well).
  • Camera — tripod, high ISO, 5–15 s; phones often see more than the eye.
