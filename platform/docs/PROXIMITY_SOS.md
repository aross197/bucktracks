# Proximity SOS (25 km) — BuckTracks

**I Am Lost** can notify:

1. **Selected people** → full GPS, back bearing, waypoints
2. **Opted-in hunters within 25 km** → **distance only** (no exact location)

The lost hunter sees nearby helpers as distances (e.g. ~4.2 km), never their map coordinates.

Full UI: `/sos` in https://github.com/aross197/NS-Deer-Paradise  
Helpers: `lib/proximity.ts`
