Advanced Whitetail Scouting &
Automated Scent Dispenser

Technical and strategic notes for patterning mature bucks — digital mapping, timber cuts, camera tradecraft, and a low-power timed attractant dispenser. Fair chase applies. Confirm all baiting and scent rules with Nova Scotia DNR before you deploy anything in the field.

1. Digital mapping & scouting platforms

  • onX Hunt — Best overall mapping & offline: aerial/topo, property lines, deep-woods cache.
  • HuntStand — Best value & organization: parcels, stands, cams, harvest logs.
  • Spartan Forge — Predictive movement & AI from collar/LiDAR-style analysis.
  • HuntWise — HuntCast / WindCast pressure and wind scoring.
  • BuckTracks — Free NS kit: Land 3D, weather, cam AI, SOS, Master Baiter (Next.js app in repo).

2. Timber cuts, crops & historical layers

  • 1–3 year cuts — Browse, briars, forbs; high-protein early-fall feed.
  • 5–10 year cuts — 6–10 ft thickets; bedding with low visibility.
  • Micro-ag pockets — Small plots and clearings pull bucks earlier than big fields.
  • Fire & storm scars — Concentrated browse away from pressure.

3. Trail cameras & natural attractants

  • High-angle cams — 7–8 ft up, angled down 30–45°.
  • Mock scrapes — Cleared earth under a licking branch on transitions.
  • Aromatic blends — Long-range fermented/sweet carriers on porous wood.
  • Cellular cams — Fewer scent loops into core cover.

4. Automated scent dispenser — parts

  • MCU: ESP32 or Arduino Pro Mini (3.3 V)
  • Pump: 3–6 V mini peristaltic
  • Power: 18650 pack + 5–10 W solar trickle
  • Switch: Logic-level N-MOSFET (e.g. IRLZ44N) or mini relay
  • Clock: DS3231 RTC
  • Box: IP67 UV-resistant enclosure

Legal note: automated attractants may be restricted. Technical reference only — not permission where prohibited in NS.

5. Sample ESP32 control sketch

Tune peak hours for your latitude and season. Prefer ESP32 light-sleep for longer battery life in production.

#include 
#include 

const int PUMP_PIN = 14; // MOSFET gate

RTC_DS3231 rtc;

void setup() {
  Serial.begin(115200);
  pinMode(PUMP_PIN, OUTPUT);
  digitalWrite(PUMP_PIN, LOW);
  if (!rtc.begin()) {
    Serial.println("Couldn't find RTC");
    while (1);
  }
}

void loop() {
  DateTime now = rtc.now();
  bool isMorningPeak = (now.hour() == 6);
  bool isEveningPeak = (now.hour() == 18);

  if (isMorningPeak || isEveningPeak) {
    Serial.println("Dispensing scent during peak window...");
    digitalWrite(PUMP_PIN, HIGH);
    delay(3000);
    digitalWrite(PUMP_PIN, LOW);
    delay(3300000); // ~55 min
  } else {
    delay(600000); // 10 min
  }
}
