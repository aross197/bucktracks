Viewing tips

  • Kp 4–5 — possible faint glow on northern horizon.
  • Kp 6+ — solid chance of visible colour.
  • Best hours — 10 pm – 2 am Atlantic.
  • Moon — new / thin crescent ideal.
  • Spot — dark northern horizon (Springhill / Oxford / Amherst).
  • Camera — tripod, high ISO, 5–15 s.
