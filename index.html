Field notes · Cumberland County

  • Wind first. Check direction before you walk in. Don’t hunt the wrong side of the ridge.
  • Access. Confirm private or Crown land and any posted boundaries before stands or cams.
  • Legal. Baiting, scent, and seasons are set by NS DNR — verify current regs every year.
  • Safety. Tell someone your plan. SOS is for a shareable fix, not a replacement for 911.
