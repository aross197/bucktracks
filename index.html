Field notes · Cumberland County

  • Wind first. Check direction before you walk in. Don’t hunt the wrong side of the ridge.
  • Access. Confirm private land / Crown land and any posted boundaries before you set stands or cams.
  • Legal. Baiting, scent, and season dates are set by NS DNR — check current regs every season.
  • Safety. Tell someone your plan. SOS page is for a shareable fix, not a replacement for 911.
